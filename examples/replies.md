# Example replies

Each example shows the other person's message, what the seller wants to say (often written in Chinese — shown here in English), and the reply. All brands, products and people are fictional.

---

## 1. Where's my order (casual)

**Them:** hey where's my order? i ordered like a week ago
**Seller wants to say:** order #1234 has shipped, about 3 days to arrive

> hey! just checked your order (#1234) and it's on its way. should be there in about 3 days. here's your tracking: [tracking link]. sorry for the wait!

---

## 2. Damaged item (formal)

**Them:** Hello, I received my mug yesterday but the lid doesn't seal properly. Coffee leaked into my bag.
**Seller wants to say:** apologize, ask for a photo, send a replacement

> Hi, thanks for letting me know. I'm really sorry, especially about your bag. Could you send me a quick photo of the lid? I want to see what's going on, and we'll get a replacement out to you right away.

---

## 3. Return request

**Them:** I'd like to return my backpack. It's not what I expected.
**Seller wants to say:** no problem, 30-day returns, fill out the form at the link

> No worries at all. You're within our 30-day return window, so you're all set. Just fill out this form and we'll take care of the rest: [return form link]. Sorry it wasn't the right fit!

---

## 4. Gift question

**Them:** Hi! My daughter just started running. Would these socks make a good birthday gift for a 15 year old?
**Seller wants to say:** yes, lots of parents buy them as gifts; recommend the pastel pack

> Hi! Definitely a good gift. We get a lot of parents buying these for their kids. For a 15 year old I'd go with the pastel pack. Same cushioning, just more fun colors. She'll love them.

## 8. Saying no (politely)

**Them:** Can you give me 50% off if I buy two?
**Seller wants to say:** can't do 50%, but there's a 15% bundle discount applied automatically

> I wish I could! 50% is more than we can do. But if you add two to your cart, 15% comes off automatically at checkout. Hope that helps a bit.
